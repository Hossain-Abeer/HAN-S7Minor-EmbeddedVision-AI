================================================================================
HOW TO APPROACH EACH SUBSECTION  (added 2026-10-28)
================================================================================
Each checkpoint (1.1, 1.2, ... 7.3) is taught in 8 blocks, in this order:

  1. Essence                — 1-3 lines: what this subsection is really about.
  2. Content & explanation  — the actual teaching. Definitions, mechanisms, why.
  3. Related supporting     — concepts from earlier lessons this depends on, and
     concepts                 concepts this will be needed for later.
  4. Summary                — 3-6 bullet points, compressed, exam-ready.
  5. Formula / definition   — a small box (3-5 lines) that can be copied verbatim
     box                      onto the Day 6 cheat sheet.
  6. Hand-calc worked       — one exam-style numeric or logical example, solved
     example                  step by step, no MATLAB.
  7. Sample-exam tag        — which sample-exam question this subsection maps to,
                              and whether it is asked as calc / explain / identify.
  8. Assessment             — 3-5 quiz questions + 1 "to do" + figures to look up
                              online and identify by name.
  9. Lesson-file TO DO      — every lesson .txt ends with a "TO DO…" block that
                              lists (a) external reading (Zhou chapters) and
                              (b) a SPECIFIC RANGE of Syllabus questions
                              (e.g. "2.1 – 2.end and 3.1 – 3.end"). These
                              ranges are mandatory and periodic — they are the
                              official self-test. Answer the FULL range listed,
                              not a hand-picked subset. Cross-check against the
                              official Answer PDF for that chapter (upload that
                              week's Answer PDF when the checkpoint opens).
                              Do NOT tick a checkpoint complete until its
                              lesson-file TO DO ranges are answered.


Block 9 maps to roadmap checkpoints like this:
  L1 .txt TO DO  →  Q 2.1–2.end, 3.1–3.end  →  checkpoint 1.4
  L2 .txt TO DO  →  Q 4.1–4.end            →  checkpoint 2.x (Day 1 tail)
  L3 .txt TO DO  →  Q 5.1–5.end, 6.1–6.end, 7.1–7.end
  L4 .txt TO DO  →  Q 8.1–8.end, 9.1–9.end
  L5 .txt TO DO  →  Q 10.1–10.end
  L6 .txt TO DO  →  Q 11.1–11.end, 12.1–12.end
  L7 .txt TO DO  →  Q 13.1–13.end

Common traps are intentionally omitted (per decision). Focus stays on positive
knowledge transfer + exam-pattern recognition.

Progress is logged by ticking the matching [ ] row in the day block below,
not by the 8 teaching blocks. Teaching blocks are conversational — they live in
the chat, not in this file.
