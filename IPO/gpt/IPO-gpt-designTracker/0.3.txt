D. CAVEATS RECORDED (non-blocking for 0.3; resolve in 0.5)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
  D-CAVEAT-1  Reference image contains cue ball.
      clothColor_noAspectRatio.png is not ball-free. Acceptable provided
      0.5 cloth sampling explicitly excludes the cue-ball ROI. DesignTracker
      0.3 wording "real cloth frame" is hereby reinterpreted as
      "cloth frame with cue ball; cue-ball ROI excluded from sampling".

  D-CAVEAT-2  Raw pixel set is a shadow/edge scan, not a clean triple.
      clothColor_rawPixelValueSet.txt reports three scanned regions:
        Region 1  Whole Cloth Box (70,77)-(1051,562):
                    H 0..174  (polluted by wood/UI wraparound)
                    S 0..255  (polluted by cue ball)
                    V 51..255
        Region 2  Cue Ball Box (848,149)-(881,182):
                    H 0..174  (mixed with cloth)
                    S 0..255  (includes white S=0)
                    V 71..255
        Region 3  Dark Shadow Box (79,558)-(87,575):
                    H 89..92
                    S 163..255
                    V 39..77
      Only Region 3 is clean, and it is shadow-only (low V). No mid-cloth
      sample exists in the file. From visual inspection, mid-cloth is
      approximately H 85..95, S 150..255, V 60..200.
      Consequence: 0.5 cannot be seeded from this file alone. 0.5 must
      either (a) manually pixel-pick the reference PNG (3-4 clicks), or
      (b) take widened Region 3 as a starting range and validate
      empirically against a real frame.

  D-CAVEAT-3  Addendum to 0.2 D4.
      0.2 D4 stated "reference frame already exists and carries the
      finalized pixel set". Correction: it carries a scan, not a finalized
      triple. 0.2 D4 remains valid in spirit (no re-sampling of frames),
      but 0.5 is responsible for converting the scan into CLOTH_H_LO/HI,
      CLOTH_S_LO/HI, CLOTH_V_LO/HI.
