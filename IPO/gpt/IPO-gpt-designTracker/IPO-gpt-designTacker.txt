================================================================================
[ ] 5.1 Rule-based if/else (not SimpleBlobDetector; not template-matching).
[ ] 5.2 Rules (evaluate in order):
        if  wr > 0.80                        -> CUE
        elif wr > 0.60                       -> UNCERTAIN
        elif wr >= 0.15                      -> STRIPE
