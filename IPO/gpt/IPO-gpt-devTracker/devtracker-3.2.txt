E. TEACHING: THE REALITY OF EDGE DENSITY
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
  Dev 3.2 confirms that while edge_density is mathematically sound, it is
  heavily influenced by two factors that the DT 4.2 assumptions underestimated:
  
  1. The Numeral Ring: Solids (1-8) are not flat colors; they have white
     circular patches with numbers. The Canny edge detector picks up this
     ring, spiking the solid ball's ed to ~0.25.
  2. The Bounding Box Boundary: Even with the 2px inset, the transition from
     the white cue ball to the green cloth creates an edge. Shadows also
     create edges. The cue ball's ed is ~0.13, not <0.04 as theorized.

  Conclusion: white_ratio (wr) is the primary, highly reliable feature for
  separating Cue (0.89) -> Stripe (0.20) -> Solid (0.05). edge_density (ed)
  is noisy and overlaps significantly between Solids and Stripes.

F. IMMINENT DT 5.2 AMENDMENTS (CRITICAL FOR DEV 3.5)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
  Observation 1: RATIO_CUE = 0.80 is too high. The cue ball is ~0.89 but can
  drop due to shadows. If it drops below 0.80, it falls into the Stripe range.
  Decision 1: Lower RATIO_CUE to 0.65 (or 0.70) during Dev 3.5.

  Observation 2: EDGE_CUE_MAX = 0.04 is completely invalid. The actual cue
  ball ed is 0.13.
  Decision 2: Raise EDGE_CUE_MAX to 0.15 during Dev 3.5. 

  Observation 3: The rubric requires classification. Because ed overlaps
  heavily between stripes and solids, ed cannot be used as the primary
  separator. It must be relegated to a tie-breaker (e.g., if wr is
  ambiguous between 0.10 and 0.15, a high ed = 0.25 pushes it to SOLID).
