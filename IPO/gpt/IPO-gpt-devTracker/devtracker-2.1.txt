C. INITIAL DEFECT — OVERLY TIGHT HSV BOUNDS (CAUGHT AND FIXED)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
  First attempt used DT 0.5 constants (H: 85-96, S: 243-255, V: 111-166).
  Result: cloth window was completely black, ball window completely white.
  Cause: The single-frame PNG sample (DT 0.5) did not account for video
  compression artifacts, dynamic lighting, and shadowing. The S_LO (243)
  was far too high, and H range (11 units) too narrow.

  Fix applied (src/config.h):
    Old: H 85..96  | S 243..255 | V 111..166
    New: H 35..100 | S  50..255 | V  50..220

  Rationale for new values:
    - Standard OpenCV green range.
    - S_LO dropped to 50 to catch shadowed cloth.
    - V_HI raised to 220 to catch brightly lit cloth near lamps.
    - H widened to 35..100 to tolerate camera white-balance shifts.
