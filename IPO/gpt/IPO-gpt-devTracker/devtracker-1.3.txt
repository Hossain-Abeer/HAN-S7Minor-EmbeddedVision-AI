E. DECISION PER DT 1.3 RULE
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
  Rule (designTracker 1.3):
    PROCESS_WIDTH = 960 (1280 if FPS > 15).

  Observation:
    FPS > 15 at 1280 (sustained ~29.8, minimum sustained ~27.7).

  Decision:
    KEEP PROCESS_WIDTH = 1280.

  Rationale (recorded for defence):
    - Higher resolution improves stripe-boundary detection (feature 4.2
      edge_density) and solid-ball numeral ring visibility.
    - Cloth mask boundary (3.1) is sampled at finer granularity, reducing
      rails/pocket bleed.
    - FPS headroom remains large (~2x the 15 threshold), so downstream
      steps (blur, mask, contours) have budget to spend.
    - Single 11.59 FPS sample is isolated, not sustained; treated as
      measurement noise.
