SCOPE
-----
Billiard ball numeral recognition only.
- In scope:  balls numbered 1-15 + cue ball (distinguished by no numeral).
- Out of scope: snooker balls (no numerals), full game rule logic, tracking,
  billing, table/pocket geometry, external integration.

Note: cue ball has no numeral, so OCR does not classify it directly.
      Cue is resolved by absence of digit + colour, handled by the parent
      project's ball-identification stage, not by OCR itself.
