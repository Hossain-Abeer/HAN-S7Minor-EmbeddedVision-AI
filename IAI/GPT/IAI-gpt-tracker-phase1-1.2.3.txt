================================================================================

DECISION
--------
Interval:  Every 5th frame.
Rationale: 30 fps x 5 clips x ~60s = ~9,000 raw frames.
           Every 5th => ~1,800 frames. Workable density for 50/class target.
           Rescale trigger: if any class under-delivers at 1.3.5, re-run at
           every 3rd frame.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
DESIGN CHOICE
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Frames are NOT dumped to disk. Extraction, filtering, and cropping live in
ONE loop; each frame is processed and discarded. Peak RAM stays flat.
This fuses 1.2.3 (extract) and 1.2.4 (filter) and 1.3 (crop) into a single
script at runtime, while keeping them logically separate in the tracker.
