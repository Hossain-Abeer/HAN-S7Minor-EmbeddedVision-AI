Demand backdrop

  - Electrical engineers in energy networks/automation: listed shortage.
  - Grid sector needs ~30,000 additional workers by 2030.
  - EU-wide: ~250,000 electrical engineers needed by 2030 for HV
    infrastructure.

Hardest roles to fill

Concrete entry-level evidence (NL)
  - Hitachi Energy (Rotterdam): Junior Technical Project Engineer,
    Grid Integration. Electromechanical design of HV systems,

    Power Systems. No years of experience listed.

    HBO diploma in electrical engineering, energy technology, or
    civil engineering. Structured onboarding, buddy system, ABP
    pension.

    Elektrotechniek required.

Salary range (entry-level NL)

Language

EE market summary

7. DEFENSE INDUSTRY - THE STRUCTURAL GATE
================================================================================

The gate, stated plainly
  - US defense: U.S. citizenship required for security clearance, without
    exception. RTX, Lockheed Martin, and every major contractor state
    this explicitly.
  - UK defense: effectively the same. MBDA requires British Citizen or
    Dual UK national with British citizenship. Some roles: "sole UK
    nationals only," no visa sponsorship.
  - Statutory, not policy preference.
  - Skills cannot substitute for citizenship.

Practical implication
  - A Dutch national cannot simply apply to Lockheed, RTX, or MBDA UK
    and be hired into a cleared engineering role.

Workarounds (three paths)

  Path A - Defense-adjacent commercial roles (no clearance required)
    - Some roles in commercial product lines, supply chain, international
      business units do not touch classified programs.
    - Rarer at US primes. More common at European primes (Thales,
      Leonardo, Saab, Airbus Defence & Space).
    - NATO nationality may help at European defense firms.

  Path B - Emigrate first, naturalise, then apply
    - Sequence: work visa -> residency -> naturalisation -> defense
      application.
    - US: H-1B (lottery, 65k + 20k master's cap).
    - UK: Skilled Worker visa. Electrical engineers on shortage
      occupation list. Going rate GBP 58,700 (experienced),
      GBP 47,100 (new entrant).
    - Timeline: 5-10 years.
    - Both routes competitive.

  Path C - Target defense sectors with lower citizenship barriers
    - Australia, Canada, New Zealand.
    - Clearance generally more accessible to Five Eyes / NATO nationals
      than US/UK.
    - Trade-off: geography and market size.

Honest plan adjustment
  - Treat "defense in US/UK" as CONDITIONAL outcome.
  - Condition = citizenship.
  - Path to condition: emigrate earlier, accept longer timeline.
  - Alternative: pivot to defense-adjacent commercial work in NL or
    elsewhere in Europe, where Dutch nationality is not a barrier and
    the EE + cyber + AI combination is already differentiated.
