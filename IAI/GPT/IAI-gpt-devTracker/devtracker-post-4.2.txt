3. WHY THIS DEFENDS "UNBIASED" IN PRELIMINARY ML §3
================================================================================

The 4.2 sort rule was: "when in doubt, delete; wrong label > missing
sample." That rule was applied uniformly to every crop, not per class.

Evidence of uniformity:
  - No class avoided deletion. Kept-to-deleted ratio was recorded per
    class only as kept counts (12-83). Deletion affected all classes.
  - The four largest deletion categories (blur, merged, HUD, clipped)
    are CLASS-AGNOSTIC. A blurry 7 is deleted by the same rule as a
    blurry 12. A merged 3+8 is deleted regardless of which numeral is
    more legible.
  - Cue balls are correctly classed as "not in OCR scope" per the 0.4
    scope lock. They are not a class, so their removal is by design,
    not by culling bias.

What the report must NOT claim:
  - "Balanced dataset." 14/15 classes fall below 50. The report states
    the imbalance as a limitation, not a success.
  - "Representative of deployment conditions." The screen-recorded
    pivot (per 1.1 change note) already limits representativeness; the
    culling adds a second limitation layer.

What the report SHOULD claim:
  - "Culling followed a single uniform rule: retain only crops with a
    fully visible, legible numeral. The 64% rejection rate reflects
    over-production by the motion gate (a recall-first design), not
    selective retention."
  - Cite this note as the breakdown artefact.

================================================================================

of the 4.2 validation). The 925 deleted crops are unrecoverable without
re-running 3.3.

too thin to stratify, the recovery path is re-extraction (3.3) with the
same motion gate, not restoration from backup. This escalation cost is
accepted in the 4.2 proceed decision.
