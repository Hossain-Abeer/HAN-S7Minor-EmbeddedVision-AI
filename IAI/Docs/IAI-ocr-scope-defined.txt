SCOPE DEFINITION — OCR SUBCOMPONENT

IN SCOPE
- Object: billiard balls numbered 1–15
- Object: cue ball (no number, distinguishes by absence of numeral + white colour)
- Task: classify ball identity from numeral on ball surface
- Ball types considered: solids (1–8), stripes (9–15), cue
- Input: cropped ball region from overhead camera frame
- Output: class label {1,2,...,15,cue}

OUT OF SCOPE
- Snooker balls (no numerals)
- 8-ball (distinguished by colour, not numeral)
- Game rule logic
- Ball tracking, motion, physics
- Table surface, pockets, rails
- Billing, display, external integration

RATIONALE
Snooker balls carry no characters → outside OCR task. Cue ball has no
numeral → treated as its own class, not via OCR but via absence of digits
+ colour. This subcomponent feeds the project's ball-identification stage,
which in turn feeds rule logic.

ASSUMPTIONS
- Overhead camera gives full-table view
- Numerals are legible at capture resolution (1080p min, 4K preferred)
- Fixed camera position and stable lighting

BOUNDARY CASES (to decide later)
- Partially occluded numerals
- Glare on ball surface
- Ball at image edge / cropped
- Wrong-colour ball (miscue)
